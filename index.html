Course Goals


1. Consider technology’s role in acts of making

Technology plays a crucial role in creating digital media, enabling different and new forms of expression. Tools such as coding environments or different design softwares/photoshop allows people to develop what they desire. I was able to toy around with these code and image options where I learned to make certain buttons do different things and lead you to different pages or outcomes. This is how I was able to make simple games and stories about what I desired (hockey).

2. Embrace experimentation

Experimentation is key to creating innovative digital media. By trying new techniques and learning different editing tactics, I was able to push my boundaries and learn new ways of creating with these first 3 assignments. I discovered just some of the potential of what I could achieve with code and design tools. I was able tocreate unique experiences for myself and my classmates such as interactive games and engaging stories.

3.Engage with others in a workshop environment

Arguably the most important part of class, the workshops, were crucial to my understanding of code. Whenever I had an issue, I would ask my friend Navaj, or my other groupmates how they overcame similar problems. There were some questions I wasn't able to answer with a simple google search that required extra collaboration. Not to mention, the feedback and examples my peers have given me have acted as motivation for my own assignments and different things I wanted to try.

4. Practice revision within and across media forms

Revision is another essential part of the creative process. By constantly reviewing and refining my work with my teachers, ai, and my classmates, I was able to improve the quality of my projects and implement new ideas that I had not yet thought of. This considerate approach allowed me to explore different ways to tell my story or create my game. For example, I could not figure out how to get my puck to shoot where I wanted it to, so I just settled and turned in a faulty version of the game. However, after collaboration, I was ablet to revise my work to create what I wanted.

5. Learn to present your work

Learning to present my work in my small group and to the entire class taught me to put my ideas into words and express how I truly went about each assignment. Explaining my assignments made me feel like a true creator, showing my classmates my game and story. Putting my work on display definitely felt redeeming and prouder of my work.
